For people scheduled this Sunday who are also in a group in Planning Center Groups.

1. List anyone whose group meets while they are scheduled to serve, with the role, the serving time and the meeting time.
2. Each one is "Worth a look": a schedule fact, not a judgment about anyone.
3. If group meetings weren't fully read, or Groups isn't part of this connection, say so.

Whether anything should change is up to the person and their leaders.

How the answer is written: the AI only picks which of these facts to show. Ozzie writes every sentence on this Mac, with people in its own order.
