For a role that still needs someone this Sunday.

1. Find the roles that still need people. If you named a role, use that one.
2. For each role, list the people on its team as the check found them: no overlap found, possible conflict, availability needs confirmation, or left out and why.
3. Keep people in the order given. It is not a ranking, and Ozzie never suggests who to ask first.
4. "No overlap found" only means nothing in Planning Center overlaps. It doesn't mean someone is free or willing; only they can say.
5. If you ask for it, prepare a draft note to one person for you to read, change and send yourself. Nothing is sent.

How the answer is written: the AI only picks which of these facts to show. Ozzie writes every sentence on this Mac, with people in its own order.
