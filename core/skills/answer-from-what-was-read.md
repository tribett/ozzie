Ozzie answers from the last check of Planning Center and nothing else.

1. Look up only what fits your question: the overview of your services, open roles, who could help with a role, pending requests, check-ins, rooms, follow-ups, group meetings, the Church rules, or what wasn't checked.
2. The AI picks the facts from those results that answer your question. People, roles and rooms reach it only as labels such as [Person 1].
3. Ozzie writes the answer on your Mac, with a fixed sentence for each fact, and puts the real names back there. If the results do not say, the answer begins with "Ozzie can't tell from what it read."
4. Anything uncertain is called "Worth a look", with the reason.
5. If you ask for a note, Ozzie prepares a draft for you to read, change and send yourself. Nothing is sent.

People are listed in no particular order. Ozzie never ranks people, and it never guesses whether someone is willing or free; only they can say. It doesn't write prayers, sermons or scripture.
