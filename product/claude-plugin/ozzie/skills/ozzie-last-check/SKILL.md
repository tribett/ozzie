# Ozzie's last check

Ozzie is a Mac app for church staff. It reads the church's Planning Center, B1 Church or Breeze on the leader's Mac and saves a copy of its last check that Claude can read, only while the leader has sharing turned on in Ozzie. Claude can read four things from Ozzie, each a fixed read of that copy:

- **Sunday readiness** (`sunday_readiness`): roles that still need people, requests without an answer, check-ins the Church rules suggest, and what the check couldn't cover.
- **Rooms, follow-ups and group meetings** (`rooms_follow_ups_groups`): room bookings that overlap, overdue follow-ups without an owner, and group meetings at the same time as someone's serving.
- **Church rules** (`church_rules`): the rules Ozzie checks against, in words, and the procedures Ozzie follows.
- **What happened** (`what_happened`): what Ozzie did recently on the leader's Mac and how it ended.

None of them asks for anything. They only read.

## How to answer

1. Read the one that fits the question. For "what needs attention" or "is Sunday covered", start with Sunday readiness. Use only these four to learn about the church's Sunday.
2. Say when the facts were checked, using the check time in Ozzie's answer, and compare it with today's date. If the check is from an earlier day, say it's a past check and that the person can check again in Ozzie. If Ozzie says the check is out of date or is a saved copy, say so plainly.
3. Keep every label exactly as Ozzie wrote it, such as [Person 1], [Role 2] or [Room 1]. Never guess, ask for or invent the name behind a label, and don't open or search files in Ozzie's own folder on this Mac to find it: Ozzie keeps the names there so they stay on the Mac. The person can see them on Ozzie's Services page. A label means the same thing only within one copy, so don't match labels from different copies.
4. Keep Ozzie's own weight on each item. "Needs a decision" is firmer than "Worth a look", and a "Worth a look" item is not a settled fact.
5. Describe schedules and tasks, never people. Don't rank or score people, and don't call anyone unreliable, at risk or drifting. Never say what a person is, believes or needs.
6. A suggested check-in is about care, not performance. Say it that way if it comes up.
7. If Ozzie says the church's system doesn't share how many people a role needs, don't call that role full or the service covered. Say what Ozzie says instead.

## What Ozzie can't do from here

Nothing Claude reads from Ozzie can check the church's system again, change anything or send anything. If the person wants someone asked, scheduled, reminded or messaged, say that they can do it themselves in their church's system, or in Ozzie, which prepares drafts for a person to send. Nothing about background checks is in Ozzie's copy. If asked, say that Ozzie doesn't share it.

## When Ozzie has nothing to share

If Ozzie says nothing is shared right now, tell the person that sharing is off in Ozzie or there's no current check, and that sharing is the leader's choice in Ozzie. Don't urge them to turn it on.

## When Claude can't reach Ozzie

If none of the four are available here:

- Ozzie for Mac needs to be installed in the Applications folder on this Mac. It comes from https://ozzie.church.
- In Cowork on this Mac and in Claude Code, this plugin starts the part of Ozzie that reads its copy.
- In Claude chat in the Claude app for Mac, the person adds Ozzie from Ozzie itself: in Settings, under Other AI apps, Spotlight and Shortcuts, they turn on "Use Ozzie's last check in other AI apps", then choose Add Ozzie to Claude.
- Claude on the web and on phones can't read Ozzie, because Ozzie's check stays on the leader's Mac.
