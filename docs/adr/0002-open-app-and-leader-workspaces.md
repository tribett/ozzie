# Open app and core, with leader-centered workspaces

Superseded in part by [ADR 0005](0005-public-export.md) (2026-11-13): the desktop app and core are both
Apache-2.0 and are published as a fresh-history public repository.

Status: accepted for the long-term product, 2026-10-23.

The owner chose each leader's workspace with selective sharing across ministries as the primary experience. The owner also chose an open-source usable desktop app and core, funded through optional managed services, over an open core with a permanently private GUI. This supersedes the long-term private-app policy in PRODUCT-SPEC section 10 and the private-shell assumption in ADR 0001; it does not approve ADR 0001's stack or change the hackathon build window. The original handoff documents retain the earlier plan for historical context.

Churches must be able to use the app and core without purchasing our managed service. Managed execution, models, and support are candidate paid services; their exact packaging, code-publication timing, and app license remain to be decided. Safety behavior belongs in the open software and must remain effective when self-hosted. Selective sharing does not grant access beyond source-system permissions or settle institutional custody, departure, and export rules; those require design.
